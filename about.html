Twenty eight years of backing people.

Gourad Group was started in 1997 and has been in the same business ever since: finding motivated operators, funding them early and staying useful afterwards.

Where we sit

We are based in Georgetown, Connecticut, and we specialize in early investment and business incubation. Starting a company is slow, expensive and frightening. Our services exist to compress that period and to take some of the financial risk off the founder.

What we underwrite

Our mission is to invest in the people behind the business. Developing management skill, team cooperation, communication and infrastructure is the most important part of what we do. The right idea in the hands of the right people is the foundation of every operation we have seen succeed.

The record

Over the years we have worked with several companies that are now independent and profitable, and we have built a network of skilled professionals along the way. Trassig Corp. and BI Works are the clearest tests of the process: both started under our supervision and both run on their own today.

The right idea worked on by the right people is the foundation of any successful operation.

Working with us

We are not a passive check. We expect to be in the room for hiring, pricing and the first serious market study. Founders who want capital and nothing else are usually better served elsewhere, and we will tell you so in the first meeting.
