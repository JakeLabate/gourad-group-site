Our process

Four steps, in order.

The sequence matters. Skipping ahead is the most common reason good ideas run out of money.

About the firm

Meet the people

We start with the founder, not the deck. Motivation, judgment and the ability to run a team are the things we are actually underwriting.

Test the market

A real study of competitors, buyers and pricing before anyone commits capital. This is where plans get sharper or get shelved.

Build the structure

Accounting, management practice, marketing plan and a workspace. Boring on purpose, and the reason companies survive year three.

Hand over the keys

Our partners graduate to independent operation. We stay in the network as investors and as the people you call when something breaks.
